PROJECT 02
Assistive Prototype AI Alcohol Sensing & Car Engine Locking System

A capstone research project developed at Holy Family Academy for the prevention of Driving Under the Influence (DUI) cases in Padre Garcia, Batangas. The system uses an MQ-3 alcohol sensor and an Arduino Uno R3 Atmega328 microcontroller to detect blood alcohol concentration via breath proximity. When alcohol is detected, a buzzer and red LED activate and the hobby gearmotor (simulating the car engine) automatically locks.
